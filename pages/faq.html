Pricing and payment

How much does the Website Sprint cost?

The Website Sprint costs $1,999, one time. There are no subscriptions or hourly charges.

When do I pay?

Only after you have seen your custom website preview and decided to go ahead. Submitting the form and receiving the preview are free.

How do I pay?

When you're ready to book, we send you a secure Stripe payment link. Stripe accepts all major cards.

Are there any hidden fees?

No. The $1,999 covers the sprint as described. Third-party costs such as domain registration, hosting plans, premium plugins or stock licenses are paid directly to those providers, and we'll tell you about any before you commit.

Can I get a refund?

Yes. If you cancel any time before kickoff, you get a full refund of the $1,999. Once kickoff happens and work on your website begins, the payment is no longer refundable.

Is there a monthly fee?

No monthly fee is required. The $499/month Care Plan is optional, offered only after launch, and you are never enrolled automatically.

Process and timeline

How long does the Website Sprint take?

Two weeks from kickoff to launch. Before that, you receive a custom preview within 24 hours of submitting your request.

What happens after I submit the form?

You get a confirmation email right away. We review your request and, within 24 hours, email you a private link to a custom website preview with the option to book your sprint.

What is the preview?

A first look at a website designed for your business, based on what you told us. It shows the direction before you spend anything.

What happens at kickoff?

Kickoff is where the sprint starts. We confirm your pages, content and goals, and the two-week build begins.

How many revisions are included?

Two revision rounds are included. Each round is a chance to review the site and request changes.

What do I need to provide?

Your business details, any copy and images you already have, your logo if you have one, and access to your domain. We'll tell you exactly what's needed at kickoff.

Who will I be working with?

The founder of Zyvibe, directly, from your first message to launch day. There are no account managers or handoffs.

Technical

Will my website work on phones?

Yes. Every site is built mobile-first and tested on phones, tablets and desktops.

Is SEO included?

On-page SEO basics are included: page titles, meta descriptions, headings, an XML sitemap, structured data, and Google Search Console setup. Ongoing SEO campaigns are not part of the sprint.

Can you build an online store or app?

Not within the standard sprint. E-commerce checkout, apps, user accounts and custom integrations are outside its scope.

Can I use my existing domain?

Yes. We connect your website to the domain you already own, or help you choose one to register.

Do you set up analytics?

Yes. Google Analytics 4 is set up so you can see how visitors find and use your site.

After launch

Who owns the website?

You do. Your domain, your content and your website belong to your business.

What is the Care Plan?

An optional $499/month plan for clients who want ongoing updates and support after launch. It's offered only after launch and never added automatically.

What if I need changes after launch?

You can make changes yourself after the handover walkthrough, ask us for a separate quote, or choose the optional Care Plan.

Can you help with more than one website?

Yes. Each website is its own sprint. Start a new request through the Website Sprint form.

Ready when you are.

Get a custom website preview within 24 hours. No payment required to see it.
