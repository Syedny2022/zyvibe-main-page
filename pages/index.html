Deliverables

What does the Website Sprint include?

Everything a small business needs to launch a credible, working website.

  • Custom design built around your brand, not an off-the-shelf template
  • The core pages your business needs, agreed at kickoff
  • Mobile-first, responsive layout for phones, tablets and desktops
  • Clear page structure and editing of the copy you provide
  • A contact or inquiry form that delivers messages to your inbox
  • On-page SEO basics: page titles, meta descriptions and headings
  • XML sitemap and robots.txt
  • Google Analytics 4 setup
  • Google Search Console setup and sitemap submission
  • Open Graph tags so links look right when shared
  • Structured data (schema markup) describing your business
  • Performance optimization, including compressed images
  • Accessibility basics: readable contrast, alt text, keyboard navigation
  • HTTPS and connection to your domain
  • Two rounds of revisions
  • Launch support and a handover walkthrough

Scope

What is outside the standard sprint?

A two-week sprint works because the scope is clear. These need a separate conversation:

  • Online stores and e-commerce checkout
  • Web or mobile apps, user accounts and dashboards
  • Custom integrations and backend systems
  • Unlimited revisions
  • Ongoing content writing or blogging
  • Logo design and full brand identity work
  • Paid advertising and ongoing SEO campaigns
  • Third-party costs such as domain registration, hosting plans, premium plugins or stock licenses
